云上小寺

Yunshang Xiaosi · a little temple in the clouds

不必去某个固定场所。打开这个网页，就可以开始一段静心之旅：走进寺院、听经、冥想，也有人轻轻陪伴。它不保证什么神奇效果，只把一段安静的时间还给你，帮你找回自己。求签与读盘仅供娱乐与自我观照。

You don’t need to go anywhere. Open this page, and a quiet visit can begin: walk through the temple, listen to a sutra, sit and breathe. It doesn’t promise mystical results—just a little time, some company, and a chance to come back to yourself. Fortune readings, if you try them, are for reflection and entertainment only.

可以做什么 / What you can do

  • 在庭院与大殿中漫步，镜头会轻轻跟在小和尚身后。
  • 打坐、听经、冥想（中英呼吸提示）、礼拜、点灯与续香。
  • 经文按真实诵读节奏同步，并按日轮换。
  • 求签处可看每日一签，也可做读盘、合盘与问答。
  • Walk the courtyard and hall with a follow camera. Sit, listen to a sutra, meditate with bilingual breath cues, bow, and light lanterns. You can also try a daily fortune slip, a birth-chart reading, a compatibility reading, or a local Q&A.
